## Music & audio

Music is more than background noise for me. I enjoy soundtracks, discovering artists, collecting records, and thinking about the gear that helps music feel right.

## Cinema & animation

I’m drawn to films that stay with you after the credits, from animation and Japanese cinema to big-screen storytelling. The work of Studio Ghibli and Makoto Shinkai has a special place on my shelf.

## Books & storytelling

I’m interested in how stories are built: the ideas behind them, the choices that shape a scene, and the details that make fictional worlds feel real. Lately, that curiosity has also nudged me toward learning more about screenwriting.

## Games & side projects

Games are a great meeting point between design, music, technology, and narrative. I also like small personal projects that let me explore an idea without turning every hobby into another job.
